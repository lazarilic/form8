Parents ask

Is the first class free?

It is. Your child does a full class with the group, then you decide.

What does my child bring to class?

Indoor trainers, a tracksuit and water. Nothing to buy up front.

Does my child need any experience?

No. Most kids start from zero and groups are split by age.

Is there a joining fee?

No. You only pay the monthly fee for the group.
